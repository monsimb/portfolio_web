Following the closure of Brazil Brasil, I wanted to start cataloging Brazilian restaurants across the Greater Boston area.

Massachusetts has a strong Brazilian community, and there are many places serving everything from casual salgados and pão de queijo to steakhouse-style churrasco, buffet-style lunch spots, and homestyle Brazilian meals. This post will be a running list of the places I try, what I recommend ordering, and how I would rate each one.

I’ll update this guide over time as I visit more restaurants.

--------


## Tag guide

`bakery` — Brazilian baked goods, coffee, pão de queijo, salgados  
`casual` — Good for a simple lunch or dinner  
`by-weight` — Buffet where you pay by plate weight  
`all-you-can-eat` — Fixed-price buffet or rodizio  
`churrascaria` — Brazilian steakhouse or grilled-meat-focused restaurant  
`snacks` — Coxinha, pastel, pão de queijo, brigadeiro, etc.  
`sit-down` — Full-service restaurant  
`takeout` — Good for pickup or quick meals
